# Instructions script

Draft for the 11/14 review (issue #51). Read it word for word in every session,
after the intro and consent form, with the app on the Welcome screen.

## Read aloud

> You'll answer four rounds of SAT-style multiple-choice questions on this laptop.
>
> For each question, an AI helper will suggest an answer. You can use its suggestion
> or ignore it.
>
> Click your answer, then click "Submit answer". You can't go back after you submit.
>
> Before and after each round, you'll rate a few things from 1 to 10. Just answer
> honestly.
>
> You can use the scratch paper, but no calculator or phone. There's no time limit.
>
> I can't answer questions once you start. Let me know when you see the "Thank you"
> screen.
>
> Any questions? If not, press "Begin".

## If they ask something during the task

> "I can't answer that during the study. Please do what you think is best."

## Notes for us

- Don't say how accurate the AI helper is.
- If the app text changes (time limit, practice question), update the matching line here.
