# Question sourcing

Record where every question in `questions.json` came from, plus our selection criteria,
so we can describe it in the report.

**Source:** [SAT Suite Educator Question Bank](https://satsuiteeducatorquestionbank.collegeboard.org/)
(filterable by section and difficulty).

**Selection criteria (draft):**
- One unambiguous correct answer, 4 choices
- Solvable without a calculator in about 1 minute
- Balanced across rounds: same mix of section and difficulty in each of sets 1-3
- Wrong-AI items: a plausible distractor exists that a confident explanation can argue for
